# Resume bullets — repo docs only

Use only with the label "Project" and only for the simulated Business Flow Improvement project described in this repo. Pick the group that matches the role; do not mix groups into one application as if they were separate projects. Do not put these bullets on the public demo site.

## Core (all roles)

- Created an end-to-end business flow improvement case for a simulated subscription product and live experience pipeline, mapping five stages from intake to release and separating waiting time from working time to locate the bottleneck.
- Analysed a simulated pipeline completing about 28 requests a week against demand of about 35, with lead time of about 21 days, rework of 22%, and quality escapes of 14%, and traced delay and rework to incomplete intake, unclear ownership, excess work in progress, late quality checks, and last-minute release changes.
- Built a phased improvement plan with one intervention per cause, and a control plan with review triggers, showing lead time falling to about 12 days, throughput rising to about 39 a week, rework to 9%, and escapes to 5% in the simulated control phase.

## Associate / Assistant Product Manager (APM)

- Created a discovery and prioritisation system for the simulated pipeline, including an intake completeness gate, priority tiers, and an impact vs. effort matrix, so that urgent member and guest impact was triaged first and low-impact work was deferred visibly.
- Analysed the request journey as a value stream, showing that working time was about 7 days while waiting time was about 14 days, and used that finding to frame the improvement as a flow problem rather than a speed problem.
- Designed the phased improvement as an experiment, with baseline measurement, one change per cause, and control-phase targets, so each change could be judged on lead time, throughput, rework, and escapes.

## Product Manager / Product Operations

- Created a launch and operations playbook for the simulated pipeline, with runbooks for launch, quality, downtime and recovery, and a daily flow review, plus a definition of done for every stage.
- Built a pipeline model with WIP limits by stage and a daily aging review, and a reporting system with fixed KPI definitions, a weekly review, and a monthly business review template.

## Business Operations / Process Improvement

- Analysed pipeline capacity in the simulated case, showing throughput rising from about 28 to about 39 requests a week at the same simulated team size, driven by falling waiting time and rework rather than added headcount.
- Created a business-model analysis showing how flow improvement affects time to value, cost to serve, quality, and predictability, with lead time range narrowing from about 18–24 days to about 10–14 days in the simulated control phase.
- Documented trade-offs in the repo: phased interventions cannot prove single-cause effects, simulated data has no staffing or seasonal variation, and four control weeks show direction, not a proven new stable level.
