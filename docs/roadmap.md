# Roadmap and schedule

## Roadmap

| Phase | Weeks | What happens | Milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Figure it out | 1–4 | Baseline measurement only. Value-stream map, Pareto, weekly table. No changes. | M1, end of Week 4: baseline agreed |
| Fix it | 5–8 | Week 5 intake gate and tiers. Week 6 owners, WIP limits, daily review. Week 7 early quality gates. Week 8 delivery checklist and freeze. | M2, end of Week 8: go / no-go to Control |
| Control it | 9–12 | No new changes. Same definitions, limits, and reviews. Weekly review, monthly business review. | M3, end of Week 12: close-out, playbook locked |

## Dependencies

- Scoping quality gate (Week 7) depends on the definition of done agreed in scoping, which depends on the intake gate (Week 5) being in place.
- WIP limits (Week 6) depend on owners being named, or there is no one to hold the limit.
- The go / no-go at end of Week 8 depends on four improve weeks of data, not on a single good week.
- Close-out at end of Week 12 depends on four control weeks, not on the improve phase alone.

## What success looks like at each milestone

- **M1:** baseline numbers agreed by all stage owners, with the same KPI definitions that will be used in every later phase.
- **M2:** all four fixes live, with playbook updates written, and a recorded go / no-go decision.
- **M3:** control averages meet the charter targets — lead under 14 days, throughput at or above demand, rework 10% or lower, escapes 6% or lower — and the playbook is locked as the new standard process.
