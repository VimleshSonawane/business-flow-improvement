# Reporting — KPIs, weekly review, and business review

## KPI definitions

All KPIs use the same definitions in every phase. Changing a definition mid-project would make the improvement unmeasurable.

- **Demand:** requests accepted at intake per week.
- **Throughput:** requests completed per week.
- **Backlog:** open requests at week end. Backlog = previous backlog + demand − completed.
- **Lead time:** days from intake accepted to release, including waiting.
- **Waiting time / working time:** waiting is time a request sits unworked in a stage; working is time actually spent on it.
- **Rework:** share of completed requests returned to an earlier stage.
- **Quality escapes:** share of releases with a defect found after release.
- **Flow efficiency:** working time divided by lead time.
- **Stage WIP:** requests open in a stage at week end, compared with that stage's WIP limit.

## Weekly review template

1. Demand vs. throughput this week, and backlog change.
2. Lead time, split into waiting and working time, by stage.
3. Rework and escapes, with the stage where each escape was created.
4. What is aging over 5 days, and who owns unblocking it.
5. One decision: what to start, what to defer, and what to stop.

## Monthly business review template

1. Phase averages: lead time, throughput, rework, escapes, backlog.
2. Capacity: throughput at current WIP limits vs. demand forecast.
3. Top three flow problems from the Pareto, and whether they moved.
4. Playbook changes made this month, and why.
5. Next experiment: what will be tested, how it will be measured, and what would make it a keep or a kill.

Reporting in this project is not a dashboard for its own sake. It is the routine that turns a pipeline into a managed operation: the same numbers, the same definitions, the same review, every week.
