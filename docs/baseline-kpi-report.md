Generated by `analysis/02_baseline.py` from `data/processed/`. Do not edit by hand; re-run the script. Measures only, no recommendations. Decisions applied: [`docs/decisions.md`](decisions.md).

**Main view: cases that started in 2018.** 2017 is shown separately and labelled; it covers two departments only, so 2017 vs 2018 is not a trend.

## Definitions

- **Year**: the start year of the case (first event, Dutch local time).
- **Submitted case**: has at least one `Declaration SUBMITTED by EMPLOYEE`.
- **Complete case**: last event is `Payment Handled` (paid) or `Declaration REJECTED by EMPLOYEE` (closed after rejection).
- **Paid case**: last event is `Payment Handled`.
- **Cycle time**: first `Declaration SUBMITTED by EMPLOYEE` to `Payment Handled`, paid cases only, in days.
- **Rejected at least once**: one or more rejections by ADMINISTRATION, PRE_APPROVER, BUDGET OWNER, SUPERVISOR or MISSING (the employee's own `REJECTED by EMPLOYEE` step is not counted as a rejection).
- **Resubmission**: any `SUBMITTED` event after the first in a case.
- **Wait before a step**: time from the previous event in the case to that step; for approver roles, any event by that role (approve or reject).
- **Percentiles**: linear interpolation (same as Excel `PERCENTILE.INC`).
- **Money**: `Amount (units as in source)`. The source states no currency. Cases with amount 0 are excluded from money figures only.

## Findings (2018 main view unless labelled)

1. **Paid cases take a median of 8.05 days from first submission to payment, but 1 in 10 takes more than 20.25 days** (P75 13.04, mean 11.62; denominator: 7,903 paid 2018 cases).
2. **Paid cases with a rejection take 1.8 times as long (median).** Median cycle time is 13.21 days for paid cases rejected at least once (815 cases) vs 7.34 days for paid cases never rejected (7,088 cases), 2018.
3. **13.14% of submitted cases are rejected at least once** (1,072 of 8,160 submitted 2018 cases). 979 resubmissions in 848 cases (10.39% of submitted cases).
4. **ADMINISTRATION does most of the rejecting**: 948 rejections, 11.62 per 100 of the 8,160 2018 cases it handled. Other roles: SUPERVISOR 2.79 per 100 (222 rejections, 7,952 cases); BUDGET OWNER 2.08 per 100 (59 rejections, 2,832 cases). PRE_APPROVER and MISSING do not appear in 2018 cases.
5. **Among the steps, the longest median wait is before `Payment Handled`.** Median wait before `Request Payment`: 1.06 days (P90 6.83; 7,896 events). Before `Payment Handled`: 3.21 days (P90 4.41). Approver waits: ADMINISTRATION 0.00 (P90 0.02); BUDGET OWNER 0.86 (P90 4.85); SUPERVISOR 1.02 (P90 6.21) days; complete 2018 cases.
6. **Payments run in batches**: 99.66% of `Payment Handled` events fall on a Monday or Thursday (7,876 of 7,903 events in 2018 cases, Dutch time): Monday 3,297, Tuesday 26, Wednesday 1, Thursday 4,579.
7. **ADMINISTRATION's first step comes a median of 41.00 seconds after submission** (9,123 transitions, complete 2018 cases; P90 31.49 minutes). This is treated as a data limitation (system-recorded), not review time (decision 5).
8. **Slowest common transitions** (median days, transitions seen in 100+ complete 2018 cases): Request Payment → Payment Handled 3.21 days (7,896 cases); Declaration APPROVED by BUDGET OWNER → Declaration FINAL_APPROVED by SUPERVISOR 1.91 days (2,784 cases); Declaration FINAL_APPROVED by SUPERVISOR → Request Payment 1.06 days (7,896 cases).
9. **The process is fairly standardised**: the most common path covers 55.81% of 2018 cases (4,610 of 8,260) and the top 10 cover 97.81%; 63 distinct paths in total. Most common path: `Declaration SUBMITTED by EMPLOYEE > Declaration APPROVED by ADMINISTRATION > Declaration FINAL_APPROVED by SUPERVISOR > Request Payment > Payment Handled`.
10. **Compliance flags** (cases with a payment, both years, denominator 10,044): payment without request 7; payment without final approval 2; payment before final approval 0; paid more than once 0; events after payment 1. Case ids are listed below and in `compliance_flags.csv`. Cases paid without final approval are control exceptions flagged for review: either a control failure or a data issue, not determinable from this data (decision 6).
11. **173 cases (1.65% of all 10,500) have no clear end** and are excluded from durations. 135 cases were saved and never submitted (2017: 35, 2018: 100); 134 of them are among the incomplete cases and 1 was paid anyway (declaration 95149). By start year and last activity: 2017 Declaration REJECTED by MISSING 30; 2017 Declaration SAVED by EMPLOYEE 34; 2018 Declaration REJECTED by ADMINISTRATION 5; 2018 Declaration REJECTED by SUPERVISOR 4; 2018 Declaration SAVED by EMPLOYEE 100.
12. **Money**: median amount 43.75 (Amount (units as in source)); cases rejected at least once hold 134645.22 of 721736.60 total (18.66%); denominator 7,907 2018 cases with amount > 0.
13. **Workload**: 2018 submissions peak in 2018-06 (1,014 SUBMITTED events) and are lowest in 2018-08 (348); see `monthly_volume.csv`.
14. *2017, two departments only, not comparable as a trend:* median cycle time 7.10 days (2,139 paid cases); 10.39% rejected at least once (229 of 2,205 submitted cases).

## Limitations

- 2017 covers two departments only (dataset description). It cannot be checked from the log, which has no department field. 2017 vs 2018 is not a trend.
- Cases are assigned to the year they started. Cases started late in 2018 may be cut off by the end of the extract (last event 2019-06-17) and appear incomplete.
- 1 paid case(s) have no SUBMITTED event and so no cycle time: declaration 95149.
- MISSING only ever rejects (its only event is `Declaration REJECTED by MISSING`), so its rate per 100 cases reaching the role can exceed 100. It is not comparable with the other roles and is left out of role charts (decision 8).
- Timestamps record when a step was completed, not when work on it started, so a "wait" includes both queue time and working time.
- ADMINISTRATION's first step comes seconds after submission. This is treated as a data limitation (system-recorded), not review time (decision 5); time spent before that step is not visible in the log.
- `org:resource` is anonymised (STAFF MEMBER / SYSTEM only), so workload per person cannot be measured.
- Amounts were anonymised by the publisher and no currency is given.
- Durations use calendar days, including weekends and holidays.
- 10 events share an exact timestamp with another event in the same case; their order follows the raw file (see `docs/data-cleaning-log.md`).
